# Resource management

## What it does
The **Resources** page (`/resources`, "Resources" in the sidebar rail) plans people's time across projects. Each person in the Space has a **weekly capacity** in hours (default 40, 0 to 168) and can be **allocated** to projects for some hours a week between two dates (no start date means already under way, no end date means ongoing). **Time off** and Space **holidays** take working days off that capacity for the weeks they fall in.
- **People tab** (default): one card per Space member, by name. A card shows their weekly capacity, a cell for each of the next eight weeks (Monday to Sunday, named by the Monday) with "allocated / that week's capacity h", a bar and a note ("10 h free", "Full", "Over by 10 h", or "No capacity" when the week's capacity is 0), then their allocations and, for admins and owners, a form to allocate them to a project. A week over capacity is marked with a warning icon, bold text, a red border and bar, so it does not rely on colour. When anyone is over capacity in the weeks shown, a notice at the top says who and in how many weeks ("Over capacity: Ada (7 weeks)").
- **Days off in a week**: a week with leave or a holiday on a working day (Monday to Friday) shows a calendar-off line under the note, "2 days off", with the reason under it: the holidays by name, then "leave" ("Founders day, leave"). Its capacity is lower in the "allocated / capacity" figure, so the bar, the free hours and the over-capacity warning use the reduced figure. A person on leave all week has capacity 0: "No capacity", or "Over by N h" when hours are still allocated.
- **Projects tab**: one card per project that has an allocation which has not finished before the first week shown, by name: hours per week in total and how many people, for each week, and the people on it with their hours and dates. Read-only; allocations are edited on the People tab.
- **Time off tab**: two cards. **Holidays** (off for everyone in the Space) and **Time off** (a person's leave) each list the entries that have not finished before the first week shown, earliest first, with the dates and how many working days they cover ("Nov 9 to Nov 13, 2026 · 5 working days"). Admins and owners add entries with a form (a holiday: name, from, until; leave: person, from, until, note), rename a holiday or edit a leave note, change either date in place, and remove an entry with the cross. "Until" is optional and means the same day. A date cleared or an end before the start is put back to the saved value and shows the reason. Members and viewers read the lists without controls and do not get the leave notes.
- **Weeks shown**: eight weeks starting with the current one. "Earlier weeks" and "Later weeks" move the window by four weeks; "This week" appears when it has been moved. Allocations, holidays and leave that finished before the first week shown are hidden from the lists (go earlier to see them).
- **Capacity and allocation editing** (admins and owners): the capacity field saves when it loses focus. An allocation's hours, start and end dates save in place; the cross removes it. A rejected value (outside 0 to 168, end before start) puts the field back to the saved value and shows the reason. The allocate form needs a project, hours above 0 and optionally an end date; "Until" empty means ongoing.
- If the Space has no project yet, admins and owners see a hint with a link to the Projects page (not on the Time off tab), and the allocate form is not shown.
- A part week counts in full: an allocation active on any day of a week adds all its hours to that week, so the page errs towards warning.
- Layout: weeks are a wrapping grid (two per row at 375px), so nothing scrolls sideways; the header, forms, allocation and time-off rows wrap; every control is at least 40px tall. Number fields have no browser spin buttons (`Input.scss`, applies app-wide).

## How days off lower capacity
- The working week is **Monday to Friday**; a weekend day never counts. Each working day off takes **one fifth of the person's weekly capacity** for that week: 40 h with two days off is 24 h; 20 h with one day off is 16 h. Capacity is not divided by the days a person usually works.
- A day that is both a holiday and the person's leave counts once, as a holiday. Overlapping leave entries count a day once. Holidays apply to everyone in the Space; leave applies to its person only.
- Allocations are not prorated: hours a week stay the same in a week with days off, which is why such a week can go over capacity. The calculation is pure, in `frontend/src/data/resource.ts` (`weekOff`, `weekCapacity`, `personLoads`).

## API and models
- `workspace_memberships.weekly_capacity_hours`: decimal(5,2), default 40, not null, validated 0 to 168 (`WorkspaceMembership::MAX_WEEKLY_HOURS`). Returned by `GET /api/v1/workspaces/:id/members` as a number. Set with `PATCH /api/v1/workspaces/:id/members/:member` and `{ weekly_capacity_hours }`, alone or together with `role` (a capacity-only change is not a role change, so an admin may set an owner's capacity). A non-numeric or out-of-range value answers 422.
- `resource_allocations` table (`ResourceAllocation`): `workspace_id`, `user_id`, `folder_id` (the project), `hours_per_week` decimal(5,2) greater than 0 and at most 168, `start_date`, `end_date` (not before the start). Validations: the person must be a member of the Space; the project must belong to the Space and be a folder with kind `project` when chosen (a project later turned back into a folder keeps its allocations, which stay editable); the Space root can never be a project. A person who leaves the Space loses their allocations there (`WorkspaceMembership#after_destroy`); deleting a project (folder) deletes its allocations; deleting the Space deletes them too.
- `/api/v1/resource_allocations` (`index`, `create`, `update`, `destroy`), scoped by `X-Workspace-Id`, body `{ resource_allocation: { user_id, folder_id, hours_per_week, start_date, end_date } }`. Errors come back as `{ errors: [...] }` with 422 ("Person must be a member of the space", "Project must be a project", ...).
- Migrations `20261101110001_add_weekly_capacity_to_workspace_memberships` and `20261101110002_create_resource_allocations`; `schema.rb` was edited by hand.
- `time_offs` table (`TimeOff`): `workspace_id`, `user_id`, `start_date`, `end_date` (both required, end not before start, both days included), `note` (string, at most 255). The person must be a member of the Space. A person who leaves the Space loses their time off there (`WorkspaceMembership#remove_resource_plan`, which also removes allocations); deleting the Space deletes it.
- `holidays` table (`Holiday`): `workspace_id`, `name` (required, at most 255), `start_date`, `end_date` (same rules as time off).
- `/api/v1/time_offs` and `/api/v1/holidays` (`index`, `create`, `update`, `destroy`), scoped by `X-Workspace-Id`, bodies `{ time_off: { user_id, start_date, end_date, note } }` and `{ holiday: { name, start_date, end_date } }`, lists in date order, errors as `{ errors: [...] }` with 422 ("Person must be a member of the space", "End date can't be before the start date", "Name can't be blank"). Both controllers include `AdminManagedList` (`app/controllers/concerns/admin_managed_list.rb`), which holds the four actions and the admin check; the models share the date rules in `DayRange`. `TimeOffBlueprint` returns `note` only to admins and owners (`null` for everyone else).
- Migrations `20261103030001_create_time_offs` and `20261103030002_create_holidays`; `schema.rb` was edited by hand again (the `version:` line was left at the higher `2026_11_03_120001`).
- Frontend: `api/resourceAllocations.ts`, `useUpdateMemberCapacity` in `api/workspaceMembers.ts` (member mutations also refresh allocations), `api/timeOffs.ts` (`TimeOffApi`) and `api/holidays.ts` (`HolidayApi`) built on `ApiResource`, types `ResourceAllocation`, `TimeOff`, `Holiday` and `WorkspaceMember.weekly_capacity_hours` in `types/index.ts`. Pure logic (weeks, per-person and per-project load, days off and reduced capacity, over-allocation, notes, input checks) is in `frontend/src/data/resource.ts`.

## Who can view and change
Everyone in the Space, including viewers, can read the plan, the holidays and who is off. **Only admins and owners can change capacities, allocations, time off and holidays** (writes need `can_manage?`; members and viewers get 403 and see no editing controls). This is the conservative choice: planning people's time is usually a manager's call, and it is a one-line change in `ResourceAllocationsController` (and the members endpoint already needs admin) if Alex wants members to edit their own capacity or allocate themselves. In a personal Space the only member is its owner, so they can plan their own time. Open product calls, built the cautious way: (1) a day off is one fifth of the weekly capacity, not capacity divided by the days a person usually works; (2) members cannot record their own leave (to allow it, relax the manage check in `TimeOffsController` for rows whose `user_id` is the signed-in user); (3) leave notes are shown only to admins and owners, since a reason can be private.

## Known limits
- Capacity is per Space, one number for every week apart from days off: no part-time schedules that change over time, and no per-day detail beyond whole days off.
- The working week is fixed at Monday to Friday; a person who works other days, or a half day off, cannot be recorded.
- Allocations are a weekly rate; a part week counts in full, and hours are not split by day. They are not reduced in a week with days off, so such a week can show as over capacity.
- Time off is not tied to a request or approval, a leave type (sick, vacation) or a calendar: there is one list, edited by admins and owners.
- Only people with a membership in the Space appear in the Time off form (not people who reach it only through a team).
- Independent of tasks: it does not read task estimates or assignees and does not compare planned allocations with actual work. The task-based Workload view (`docs/backlog/workload-view.md`, branch `backlog/workload-view`, needs task estimates) has its own single capacity; see `docs/backlog/workload-uses-resource-capacity.md` for joining the two (it would use the weekly capacity from `personLoads`).
- Only active projects can be chosen; an allocation to a project that was archived shows as "Archived project".
- Allocations to a project cannot be created from the project's page, only from the People tab. There is no "who is free" search, no export, and no history of changes.
- Date fields in the allocation rows still use the browser's date input and save as soon as a date is picked; hours save when the field loses focus. The new Time off tab uses the styled `DatePicker`.
